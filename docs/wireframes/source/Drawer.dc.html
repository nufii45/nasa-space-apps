Rover trip

[ 3D close-up of the rover, slowly turning ]

Drive the rover far away to study rocks. You earn lots of science, but you are far from the Shelter.

This job is outside. Out there you get more radiation (invisible energy from the Sun and space that is bad for the body), and storms hit hardest.

Only one astronaut can drive the rover each sol.
